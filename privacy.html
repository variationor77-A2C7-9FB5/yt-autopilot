Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

YT Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed through Google

  • YouTube upload — to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel.
  • YouTube read-only — to read the channel's own videos, statistics and public comments.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only — to read the channel's own performance reports.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how the channel performs.
  • Access tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private server, readable only by the tool.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The tool never replies to comments or acts on other people's accounts.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted when the tool is removed from the server.

YT Autopilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Инструмент загружает ролики владельца на его канал и читает статистику этого канала. Данные хранятся на личном сервере владельца, никому не передаются и не продаются. Отозвать доступ можно в любой момент на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
